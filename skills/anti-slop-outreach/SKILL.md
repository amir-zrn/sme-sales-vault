# Anti-Slop Cold Outreach

This skill writes cold outreach (cold emails, personalized first touches, and multi-channel openers) for SME founders, technical operators, and boutique agency owners. It replaces automated SDR spam with peer-to-peer, evidence-backed notes that bypass the prospect's mental spam filter and drive replies.

---

## 1. Core Principles

Every piece of copy generated by this skill obeys five non-negotiable rules:

1. **Lane C Casual Lowercase (Default)**:
   - Use casual, all-lowercase text for subject lines, body copy, and CTAs.
   - It reads as typed on a phone or laptop keyboard in 45 seconds by an equal peer, not rendered by a cold email sequencer or marketing automation platform.
   - Formal sentence case (Lane A) is reserved only for blunt 1-line verdicts or formal proposals.
   - Keep technical acronyms standard (`AI`, `API`, `CRM`, `JSON`, `SaaS`, `AWS`). Keep product names recognizable (`n8n`, `Make`, `Postgres`, `Shopify`).
2. **First-Person Builder Authority**:
   - Write from the perspective of someone who builds and operates systems, not an outsourced SDR pitching appointments.
   - Lead with concrete receipts: real mechanisms, specific numbers, architecture decisions, and operational details.
3. **The Portability Test**:
   - If a sentence could be copied and sent to 10 other founders in the same industry without changing a word, **it fails immediately**.
   - Every message must contain at least one verifiable fact unique to that exact prospect (a technical choice, a recent hire, a specific UI flow, an API limitation, a comment they made).
4. **Zero Puffery & Zero AI Tells**:
   - No synthetic enthusiasm ("thrilled to connect", "super impressive").
   - No throat-clearing ("hope this email finds you well", "my name is X and I help Y").
   - No binary contrasts ("it's not X, it's Y") or dramatic fragment drops ("period.", "that's it.").
5. **The Asymmetric Value Offer (Risk-Reversal)**:
   - Cold emails fail when they ask for a 30-minute calendar block from a stranger.
   - The initial ask must be frictionless: offer a free sample, a 1-page architecture teardown, a 60-second unlisted Loom, or a zero-risk guarantee before asking for time.

## 3. Strict Banned List & Anti-Patterns

### Banned Vocabulary
Never use these words or their derivatives in any outreach draft:
> `delve`, `tapestry`, `testament`, `transformative`, `elevate`, `harness`, `seamless`, `seamlessly`, `dynamic`, `supercharge`, `unlock`, `game-changer`, `paradigm`, `empower`, `foster`, `resonate`, `synergize`, `cutting-edge`, `revolutionize`, `leverage`, `utilize`, `robust`, `streamline`, `meticulous`, `pivotal`, `crucial`, `underscore`, `garner`, `showcase`, `bolster`, `nuanced`, `comprehensive`, `landscape`, `realm`, `journey`, `ecosystem`, `embark`, `paramount`, `facilitate`.

### Banned Connectors & Fillers
> `furthermore`, `moreover`, `additionally`, `thus`, `hence`, `in order to`, `when it comes to`, `at the end of the day`, `it's worth noting`, `in conclusion`, `last but not least`, `let's dive in`.

### Banned Deliverability & Spam Triggers
> `100% free`, `risk-free`, `guaranteed revenue`, `exclusive deal`, `buy now`, `act fast`, `special promotion`, `make money`, `urgent`.

### Structural Anti-Patterns
1. **The Throat-Clearer**: Starting with greetings that waste screen real estate on mobile push notifications ("hi [name], hope you are having a productive week").
2. **The Binary Contrast**: Framing insights as "it's not about leads, it's about conversion" or "not just X, but Y". State the mechanism directly.
3. **The Colon Reveal**: Writing "the best part: it works" or "the result: 40% growth". Use plain prose.
4. **The Unearned Meeting Ask**: Asking "when are you free for a 15-minute introductory call next week?" on cold contact.
5. **The Rule-of-Three Cadence**: Forcing adjectives or verbs into groups of three for poetic rhythm.

## 4. Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

When writing cold outreach for a prospect, execute this 5-step pipeline:

```
[Prospect Data] 
       ↓ 
1. Scrape & Receipt Discovery (Extract 1 unique fact)
       ↓
2. Identity & Social Proof (1 sentence: peer credential + number)
       ↓
3. The Offer / Free Artifact (Deliver value upfront, zero pitch)
       ↓
4. Frictionless CTA (Soft call-to-interest or direct time slot)
       ↓
5. Mandatory Anti-Slop Audit Checklist (Pass / Fail)
```

### Step 1: Scrape & Receipt Discovery
Analyze the prospect's company website, tech stack, job listings, recent social posts, or product UI. Find one specific friction point or observation:
- A specific webhook or API challenge they mention in a hiring post.
- A manual step in their current customer onboarding or checkout flow.
- A public milestone (e.g., hitting $50k MRR, opening a second warehouse).
- An exact tooling choice (`Next.js`, `Shopify Plus`, `PostgreSQL`, `HubSpot`).

### Step 2: Establish Identity & Authority
Answer "who are you and why do I care?" in one sentence. Include a specific peer metric:
- *"i run a similar automation pipeline for an istanbul furniture brand doing $400k/mo."*
- *"i build backend data scrapers for 3 b2b marketplaces right now."*

### Step 3: Package the Offer (Asymmetric Value)
Propose an artifact or sample that requires zero commitment from them:
- A free 1-page architecture breakdown.
- A 48-hour free sample workflow or data scrape on their public data.
- A 2-minute unlisted screen recording pointing out the leak.

### Step 4: Frictionless Single CTA
End with a low-friction question or a specific, concrete time option:
- *"open to seeing the 1-page breakdown?"*
- *"happy to send the loom over if you want it."*
- *"can ring you at 10am tomorrow or thursday if worth exploring."*

### Step 5: The Mandatory Audit Checklist
Run this audit on the final draft before sending:
- [ ] Is it written in Lane C (all lowercase) or Lane A (blunt sentence case)?
- [ ] Is the subject line $\le 4$ words and devoid of sales pitch?
- [ ] Is the entire email $\le 90$ words (or connection note $\le 280$ characters)?
- [ ] Did it pass the Portability Test (contains a fact unique to this prospect)?
- [ ] Is it free of all banned words and structural anti-patterns?
- [ ] Is there zero meeting pressure in the first touch?

## 5. The 3-Touch Multi-Channel Cadence

### Day 1: The Receipt-Driven First Touch
- **Channel**: Cold Email or LinkedIn Connection Note.
- **Goal**: Establish peer relevance, prove you did homework, offer free sample.
- **Word Budget**: 50–75 words (Email) or $\le 280$ characters (LinkedIn).

### Day 3: The Value-Drop Asset / Micro-Follow-up
- **Channel**: Reply in the same email thread or LinkedIn message after connection acceptance.
- **Goal**: Drop the promised value asset or reference the exact mechanism without guilt-tripping.
- **Word Budget**: 25–45 words.

### Day 7: The Blunt 1-Line Bump / Breakup
- **Channel**: Reply in the same thread.
- **Goal**: Force a clean yes/no with deadpan honesty. No drama, no "bubbling this up".
- **Word Budget**: 10–25 words.

## 6. Production Message Templates

### Template 1: Founder-to-Founder Cold Email (Day 1)
```text
subject: quick question on your [specific process/tool]

hey [first name],

saw you handle [specific process, e.g. dealer onboarding / ad creative testing] in-house at [company].

i run that exact backend pipeline for a [niche] brand right now (saving about 15 hours a week on manual syncs). noticed your [specific public detail, e.g. multi-step lead form / catalog update] and spotted one spot where leads probably drop off.

put together a 1-page diagram showing how we fixed it. happy to send it over if useful. open to it?
```

### Template 2: Technical Operator Cold Email (Day 1)
```text
subject: [company] + [specific api/tool]

hey [first name],

read your note on hiring a backend engineer for your [tool/pipeline].

i build custom data pipelines for b2b teams (mostly n8n + postgres). last month we cut sync latency from 4 hours to 30 seconds for a team dealing with the same webhook drops.

happy to build a sample script on your test endpoint for free so you can evaluate the work directly. 

worth a quick look?
```

### Template 3: LinkedIn Connection Request Note (Day 1 — Max 280 Characters)
```text
saw your post on n8n webhook evals. i run a similar sync engine for client lead routing. would like to follow your work here.
```
*(Character count: 122 — passes easily)*

### Template 4: Value-Drop Follow-up (Day 3)
```text
hey [first name],

recorded a 90-second loom showing the exact webhook routing setup we use to prevent drops. 

no pitch or prep needed. want me to drop the link here?
```

### Template 5: Deadpan Bump / Breakup (Day 7)
```text
hey [first name],

still happy to send that 1-page breakdown over if it helps with [problem]. 

if your hands are full right now, no worries at all. want it?
```

## 7. Real Before & After Audits

### Case 1: E-Commerce Ops Outreach
- **Before (AI Slop / Generic SDR)**:
  > Subject: Revolutionize Your E-commerce Operations With AI-Powered Automation
  >
  > Dear Sarah,
  >
  > I hope this email finds you well! In today's fast-paced digital landscape, e-commerce brands like FurniCraft need cutting-edge solutions to supercharge their growth. At NextGen Solutions, we empower retailers to unlock seamless multi-channel inventory management and elevate customer satisfaction.
  >
  > Our transformative platform leverages AI to streamline your supply chain. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call this Thursday at 2 PM to explore synergies?
  >
  > Best regards,  
  > Alex
- **Audit Findings**:
  - Contains 8 banned words (`revolutionize`, `landscape`, `cutting-edge`, `supercharge`, `empower`, `unlock`, `seamless`, `elevate`, `transformative`, `leverage`, `streamline`).
  - Throat-clearing opening line.
  - Zero receipts or specific facts about FurniCraft.
  - Asks for a 15-minute call with zero value delivered.
- **After (Anti-Slop Lane C)**:
  > subject: furnicraft dealer stock sync
  >
  > hey sarah,
  >
  > saw you handle stock updates across 4 showroom locations manually. i run an automated inventory sync for an istanbul furniture brand with 6 showrooms, cutting update lag from 24 hours to 2 minutes.
  >
  > drew a quick 1-page schema of the exact webhook flow we used. happy to send it over if you want to steal the architecture for your team.
  >
  > open to seeing it?

### Case 2: B2B LinkedIn Outreach
- **Before (AI Slop / Pitch Deck in DM)**:
  > Hi Marcus, I hope you are having a wonderful week! I came across your profile and was very impressed by your journey scaling CloudScale. It's not just about cloud architecture; it's about driving transformative digital change. We help companies like yours supercharge database queries. When would be a great time for a brief 20-minute chat?
- **Audit Findings**:
  - Binary contrast ("it's not just about X, it's about Y").
  - Flattery puffery ("very impressed by your journey").
  - Banned words (`transformative`, `supercharge`).
  - No proof point.
- **After (Anti-Slop Lane C Connection Note)**:
  > hey marcus, saw your post on partitioning multi-tenant postgres tables. we ran into the exact same connection pool bottleneck at 10k users last month. wanted to connect and follow how you solved it.

### Case 3: Agency Retargeting Outreach

  - Throat clearing ("let's be honest:").
  - Banned words (`utilize`, `dynamic`, `bolster`).

  > hey david,
