# DM Sequencer (LinkedIn Social Cadence)

This skill designs and executes high-converting, human LinkedIn touchpoint sequences for SME founders and operators. It eliminates the robotic patterns of automated outreach tools (instant post-connect pitch blasts, fake pleasantries, "bumping this up" guilt trips) and enforces conversational, peer-level rapport that drives replies.

---

## 1. Core Principles

Every DM sequence generated by this skill adheres to five foundational standards:

1. **Micro-Commitments Over Meeting Asks**:
   - Never paste a calendar link or ask for a 15–30 minute meeting in the first two touches.
   - The first milestone is generating a conversational reply or permission to send an asset ("open to seeing the 1-page breakdown?").
2. **Lane C Casual Lowercase & iPhone Pacing**:
   - Write in casual, all-lowercase text. Keep sentences short (under 15 words).
   - The DM must look like it was typed on a smartphone between meetings, not templated in an outbound sequencing platform.
   - Never use bullet points, bold mid-sentence text, or greeting pleasantries inside a social DM.
3. **Signal-Driven State Transitions (No Blind Blasts)**:
   - Cadences advance based on prospect behavior, not rigid mindless timers.
   - If a prospect accepts a connection, **enforce a 24–48 hour delay** before sending a message. Blasting a DM within 60 seconds of acceptance immediately signals bot automation.
4. **Radical Brevity**:
   - Connection notes: $\le 280$ characters.
   - Follow-up DMs: 1–3 sentences max (under 45 words).
5. **Defense Against 2026 AI Slop Filters**:
   - LinkedIn flags repetitive templates and automated language patterns. This skill rotates phrasing, varies structure, and strips out synthetic AI tells and performed enthusiasm.

## 3. Strict Banned List & Anti-Patterns

### Banned Openers & Greetings
Never start a LinkedIn DM with any of the following:
> `"Thanks for connecting!"`, `"Great to connect with you!"`, `"I noticed we share mutual connections"`, `"Hope your week is off to a great start!"`, `"Hope you are doing well"`, `"Quick question for you"`, `"I came across your profile and was impressed"`.

### Banned Follow-up Clichés
> `"Just bumping this to the top of your inbox"`, `"Did you get a chance to see my last message?"`, `"Just following up on this"`, `"Circling back on this"`, `"I know you're super busy, but..."`, `"Touching base"`.

### Banned Vocabulary
> `synergy`, `streamline`, `leverage`, `seamless`, `unlock`, `supercharge`, `disrupt`, `scalable`, `game-changer`, `paradigm`, `elevate`, `harness`, `transformative`, `robust`, `endeavor`.

### Structural Anti-Patterns
1. **The Post-Acceptance Ambush**: Pitching your company, product, or Calendly link 2 minutes after someone accepts your connection request.
2. **The Wall of Text**: Sending 4 paragraphs in a single DM message that requires scrolling on a mobile device.
3. **The Fake Compliment**: "Love what you are doing at [Company]" without citing an actual post, decision, or metric.
4. **The Guilt-Trip Bump**: Passive-aggressive follow-ups complaining that the prospect didn't respond.

## 4. Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

```
[Target Prospect]
       ↓
1. Signal & Angle Extraction (Inspect recent post, headline, bio)
       ↓
2. Touch 1: Connection Request Note (≤280 chars, peer observation)
       ↓
3. Acceptance Wait (Mandatory 24–48h delay)
       ↓
4. Touch 2: Value-Drop DM (Specific problem + free sample/asset)
       ↓
5. Touch 3: Day 7 Deadpan Bump (1-line zero-friction check)
       ↓
6. Touch 4: Day 10 Polite Breakup (Permission to close file)
```

### Step 1: Signal & Angle Extraction
Inspect the prospect's profile and recent feed activity. Select one of three angles:
- **Angle A: The Technical / Stack Mirror**: You both build on similar tooling (`Shopify Plus`, `Postgres`, `n8n`, `HubSpot`).
- **Angle B: The Content Observer**: You read an exact post or comment they made within the last 30 days and have a technical comment.
- **Angle C: The Operational Bottleneck**: You run the exact operational workflow they are currently trying to hire or build.

### Step 2: Touch 1 — Connection Request Note (Day 1)
Draft a note strictly under 280 characters.
- Must cite the specific angle extracted in Step 1.
- No sales pitch. No calendar ask. No "would love to learn more about what you do".

### Step 3: Enforce the Buffer (Day 1.5–2)
When the connection is accepted:
- **Do nothing for at least 24 hours**. 
- If the prospect replies to the connection note directly, answer conversationally in real time. If they only accept, wait for the Day 3 window.

### Step 4: Touch 2 — First DM / Value Drop (Day 3)
Send a short, 2–3 line DM offering an asset or observation:
- Acknowledge their specific domain.
- State a concrete receipt or number from your own work.
- Offer the asset without requiring a phone call first.

### Step 5: Touch 3 — Deadpan Bump (Day 7)
If no reply by Day 7:
- Send a 1-sentence check-in.
- Keep tone light, helpful, and deadpan. No guilt.

### Step 6: Touch 4 — Clean Breakup (Day 10)
If still no reply after Day 7:
- Send a clean 1-line closing message.
- Remove all pressure. Give them an easy out.

## 5. Production Message Templates

### Stage 1: Connection Request Notes (Day 1 — Max 280 Characters)

#### Template 1A: The Technical Mirror
```text
saw your post on n8n webhook evals. i run a similar sync engine for client lead routing. would like to follow your work here.
```

#### Template 1B: The Content Observer
```text
your breakdown on reducing checkout drop-off was spot on, especially point 2 on address autofill. following along.
```

#### Template 1C: The Operational Peer
```text
saw you manage wholesale dealer onboarding at [company]. i handle that workflow for an istanbul furniture brand. good to connect.
```

---

### Stage 2: Value-Drop First DM (Day 3 — After Acceptance)

#### Template 2A: The 1-Page Architecture Drop
```text
hey [first name],

saw you guys run dealer stock updates in-house. 

built a 1-page schema last week showing how we automated stock sync across 6 showrooms with zero webhook lag. happy to drop the link here if you want to steal the setup for your team.

want it?
```

#### Template 2B: The 60-Second Video Audit
```text
hey [first name],

noticed your lead form on the mobile checkout has 4 extra fields that usually tank conversion on mobile traffic.

recorded a quick 60-second screen teardown showing what we changed for a similar store to bump completions by 18%. 

no pitch, happy to send the loom over if you're curious. open to it?
```

---

### Stage 3: The Deadpan Follow-up (Day 7)

#### Template 3A: The Closed-Loop Question
```text
hey [first name],

still happy to send that 1-page schema over if it helps with [specific problem]. 

if your hands are full right now, no worries at all. want me to shoot it over?
```

#### Template 3B: The Direct Alternative
```text
hey [first name],

following up on this—if you already solved the [specific bottleneck] in-house, feel free to ignore. otherwise happy to share the flow.
```

---

### Stage 4: The Clean Breakup (Day 10)

#### Template 4A: Graceful Close
```text
hey [first name],

guessing this isn't a priority right now. closing the loop so i don't crowd your inbox. cheers.
```

---

### Stage 5: In-Flight Objection Handlers (Live DM Replies)

#### Scenario A: Prospect asks "How much does it cost?"
```text
depends on the exact integration scope, but usually ranges between $1.5k and $3k one-time. happy to send the 1-page scope first so you can see the deliverables before talking budget.
```

#### Scenario B: Prospect asks "Is this an automated bot?"
```text
no, typed this between client deploys. saw your post on postgres indexing last week and meant it.
```

#### Scenario C: Prospect says "Not interested right now"
```text
totally get it, thanks for letting me know. keep shipping.
```

#### Scenario D: Prospect says "Sure, send it over"
```text
[drops direct un-gated URL or Loom link]

here's the link: [url]

curious to hear if that address autofill part maps to what your devs are seeing.
```

## 6. Real Before & After Audits

### Case 1: Post-Connection Welcome Message
- **Before (Automated SDR Bot Pitch)**:
  > Hi Mark! Thanks so much for connecting with me on LinkedIn! I hope you are having an amazing week so far. 
  > 
  > I help enterprise businesses supercharge their pipeline by leveraging AI-driven omnichannel outreach. We empower founders to unlock 10x ROI.
  > 
  > Are you available for a quick 15-minute intro call this Tuesday at 10 AM EST? Here is my Calendly link: calendly.com/spam-link. Looking forward to speaking!
- **Audit Findings**:
  - Sent immediately after connection acceptance (bot giveaway).
  - Contains banned words (`supercharge`, `leverage`, `empower`, `unlock`).
  - Includes an unearned Calendly link in touch 1.
  - Exclamation mark overload.
- **After (Anti-Slop Lane C)**:
  *(Sent 36 hours post-acceptance)*
  > hey mark,
  >
  > saw you run outbound for the uk sales team in-house. 
  >
  > i built an automated lead enrichment scraper for a b2b client that cut manual research time from 3 hours a day to zero. put together a 1-page diagram of the flow.
  >
  > happy to drop the link here if you want to check it out. open to it?

### Case 2: Follow-up Nudge (Day 7)
- **Before (Guilt-Trip Follow-up)**:
  > Hi Mark, Just bumping this to the top of your inbox! I know how busy things get at high-growth companies. Did you have a moment to review my previous message regarding our AI automation platform? Would love to grab 10 minutes on your calendar tomorrow!
- **Audit Findings**:
  - Uses classic banned throat-clearing ("bumping this to the top of your inbox", "I know how busy things get").
  - Asks for a calendar call again despite zero interest.
- **After (Anti-Slop Lane C)**:
  > hey mark,
  >
  > still happy to drop that 1-page flow over if it helps your reps. 
  >
  > if your plate is full right now, no stress. want it?

### Case 3: Connection Request Note
- **Before (Generic Connection Spam)**:
  > Hello Sarah, I came across your profile and was very impressed by your work in logistics. I would love to connect with like-minded professionals in the tech and supply chain ecosystem! Let's connect!
- **Audit Findings**:
  - Generic praise without substance.
  - Banned words (`ecosystem`, `like-minded professionals`).
  - Fails the Portability Test completely.
- **After (Anti-Slop Lane C)**:
  > saw your update on moving the warehouse inventory to barcode scanning. we ran that transition in an istanbul factory last winter. wanted to follow your updates.
