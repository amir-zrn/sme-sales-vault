# Rapid Proposal Writer

## When to Use This Skill

Activate this skill when:
- The user finishes a sales call or discovery meeting and needs a proposal sent within hours
- Raw discovery notes, call transcripts, or messy bullet points need to be converted into a structured scope of work
- The user wants a high-conversion, 1-page proposal instead of a 20-page slide deck
- Triggers: "write proposal from notes", "turn call notes into scope", "create 1-page proposal", "draft statement of work"

## Core Principles

1. **The 24-Hour Velocity Rule.** Proposals sent within 24 hours of a discovery call close at 2x the rate of proposals sent after 48 hours. When momentum fades, deals stall.
2. **Never pitch in the proposal.** A proposal is a confirmation of what was already agreed upon, not a sales pitch. If you are introducing new ideas the client hasn't heard, the deal isn't ready for a proposal.
3. **Use the prospect's exact language.** Mirror their exact words for their problems, systems, and metrics. It builds instant rapport and proves you listened.
4. **Three-option modular pricing.** Always present three options (Core, Turnkey, Accelerated). This shifts the buyer's internal debate from *"Should we hire them?"* (Yes/No) to *"Which option fits us best?"* (Choice).
5. **Enforce negative scope.** Clearly stating what is *not* included protects project margins and eliminates scope creep before work starts.

## The 1-Page Modular Proposal Architecture

A standard proposal must fit on a single page (or a short Notion/Doc view) with these 5 sections:

### Section 1: Situation Summary & Context
2–3 punchy sentences summarizing where the client is today, the core bottleneck, and why solving it now is critical. Write this in their words.

### Section 2: Objectives & Measurable Success Metrics
Bullet points defining business outcomes—not activities.
- *Bad:* "Build 4 automated Zapier workflows and write documentation."
- *Good:* "Reduce manual order-routing time from 15 hours/week to under 30 minutes/week with zero dropped orders."

### Section 3: Scope Options (The 3-Tier Matrix)
- **Option 1: Core Foundation (The Budget Shield).** Minimal viable scope that solves the primary bottleneck. Ideal if budget is tight.
- **Option 2: Turnkey Implementation (Recommended).** Complete, end-to-end execution. Solves the main problem and provides complete testing and team training.
- **Option 3: Accelerated / Premium (The Anchor).** Turnkey execution plus expedited delivery, white-glove migration, and 60 days of priority advisory/optimization.

### Section 4: Boundaries & Explicit Exclusions (Negative Scope)
A bulleted list of work explicitly *not* covered under this scope. Kills scope creep permanently.

### Section 5: Investment, Terms & 1-Click Acceptance
Clear pricing table, payment schedule (e.g., 50% upfront / 50% upon delivery), and a 1-sentence acceptance confirmation.

## 3-Minute Conversion Workflow

When raw call notes are provided:

1. **Extract 4 Critical Variables:**
   - **Pain point / Current cost:** What is breaking and what is it costing them weekly?
   - **Target metric:** How will the client evaluate success in 30–90 days?
   - **Deadline / Urgency driver:** Is there a product launch, board meeting, or fiscal deadline?
   - **Client vocabulary:** Specific names of their internal tools, team roles, or customer segments.
2. **Draft Section 1 & 2 using client phrases:** Keep it focused entirely on business results.
3. **Structure the 3 Options:**
   - Option 1 = 60–70% of Option 2 price.
   - Option 2 = Target deal size (highlighted as Recommended).
   - Option 3 = 1.5x–2x of Option 2 price (acts as a psychological anchor).
4. **Define 3–4 explicit exclusions:** Items commonly assumed to be included that take outsized time (e.g., historical data cleanup, third-party software subscription fees, custom copy generation).
5. **Format as clean Markdown:** Output ready to paste into an email, Google Doc, or Notion page.

## Anti-Slop Writing Rules

1. **Delete all corporate throat-clearing:**
   - Banned: "We are thrilled to present this proposal for your consideration."
   - Banned: "Our agency leverages cutting-edge industry best practices."
   - Banned: "We pride ourselves on delivering bespoke end-to-end synergies."
2. **Focus on outcomes, not labor:** Do not list hours worked or task checklists. List completed states.
3. **No self-indulgent company history:** Zero paragraphs about when your company was founded, your core values, or your mission statement. The client only cares about their own problem.
4. **Tone:** Crisp, confident, technical, and commercial. Write like an executive summary.

## Proposal Template

```markdown
# [Project Name] — Scope & Implementation Options
**Prepared for:** [Client Name], [Company]
**Date:** [Date] | **Valid until:** [Date + 14 days]

---

### 1. Situation Summary
[Company] is currently experiencing [core operational bottleneck / revenue leak], requiring [hours per week / lost revenue]. With [upcoming deadline / company goal], the objective is to deploy a reliable solution that eliminates [pain point] without adding headcount or distracting core engineering.

### 2. Measurable Objectives
- **Metric 1:** [Specific measurable goal, e.g., Cut lead-to-response time from 4 hours to under 2 minutes]
- **Metric 2:** [Specific measurable goal, e.g., Eliminate 100% of manual data entry errors between CRM and ERP]
- **Metric 3:** [Target operational go-live date, e.g., Full production rollout by November 15]

---

### 3. Implementation Options

| Feature / Deliverable | Option 1: Core | Option 2: Turnkey (Recommended) | Option 3: Accelerated |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Core Deliverable A] | Included | Included | Included |
| [Core Deliverable B] | Included | Included | Included |
| [Integration / Advanced Module] | Client self-setup | Included (Full Setup) | Included (Full Setup) |
| [Data Migration / Historical cleanup] | Excluded | Up to 10k records | Unlimited records |
| [Team Training & Documentation] | Loom video only | Live workshop + SOPs | Live workshop + dedicated Slack support |
| [Delivery Timeline] | 4 weeks | 3 weeks | 10 business days |
| **Total Investment** | **$[Price 1]** | **$[Price 2]** | **$[Price 3]** |

---

### 4. Explicit Exclusions (Negative Scope)
To ensure delivery speed and protect project timelines, the following items are outside the scope of this engagement:
- Third-party software subscription fees (e.g., tool licenses, API usage fees).
- Cleaning up corrupted or duplicate historical records prior to [date].
- Any platform or API integrations not explicitly listed in Section 3.

---

### 5. Terms & Acceptance
- **Payment Structure:** 50% upon kickoff, 50% upon final production sign-off.
- **Next Step:** To approve, reply to this document with your selected option (Option 1, 2, or 3) and billing details. We will issue the agreement and schedule kickoff within 24 hours.
```

## Realistic Examples

### Input: Raw Call Notes
```text
Call with Jason Miller (Co-Founder, GearVault - Shopify store doing $4M/yr)
- Right now returns are a complete nightmare. 200 returns/week handled manually in Gorgias and Google Sheets.
- Support reps spend ~25 hours a week just issuing return labels and updating inventory.
- Big complaint: Customers mad because refunds take 6-8 days. Want refunds down to 24 hrs.
- They use Shopify Plus, Gorgias, and ShipStation.
- Black Friday is in 8 weeks, Jason terrified the system will collapse during Q4 volume.
- Budget ballpark mentioned: around $10k-$15k if it fixes the problem before November.
```

### Generated Output: 1-Page Proposal

```markdown
# GearVault Returns Automation — Project Scope & Options
**Prepared for:** Jason Miller, Co-Founder, GearVault  
**Date:** September 27, 2026 | **Valid until:** October 11, 2026  

---

### 1. Situation Summary
GearVault currently processes ~200 returns per week manually across Gorgias and Google Sheets, consuming 25 hours of support time each week and causing customer refund delays of 6–8 days. With Q4 holiday volume approaching in 8 weeks, the current manual process threatens to bottleneck customer support and suppress repeat purchases during peak season.

### 2. Measurable Objectives
- **Automate Return Processing:** Reduce weekly manual support hours spent on returns from 25 hours to under 2 hours.
- **Accelerate Refund Speed:** Drop customer refund turnaround time from 7 days to under 24 hours upon warehouse receipt.
- **Holiday Readiness:** Full production testing and rollout completed 4 weeks before Black Friday.

---

### 3. Implementation Options

| Feature / Deliverable | Option 1: Core Sync | Option 2: Turnkey Automation (Recommended) | Option 3: Q4 Rush & Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shopify + ShipStation Return Portal** | Included | Included | Included |
| **Automated Label Generation** | Customer self-serve | Customer self-serve | Customer self-serve + QR code returns |
| **Gorgias Support Integration** | Basic ticket tagging | Full macro automation & status sync | Full macro automation + auto-closing resolved tickets |
| **Inventory & Restock Automation** | Excluded (manual) | Automated Shopify restock trigger | Automated restock trigger + warehouse alert routing |
| **Support Team Training** | 1 recorded Loom SOP | 1 live staff workshop + written SOP | 2 live workshops + dedicated Q4 Slack channel (30 days) |
| **Timeline** | 3 weeks | 2 weeks | 7 business days |
| **Total Investment** | **$8,500** | **$13,500** | **$18,000** |

---

### 4. Explicit Exclusions (Negative Scope)
- Monthly subscription fees for Shopify apps, ShipStation, or Gorgias.
- Physical warehouse barcode scanner hardware or setup.
- Processing or resolving dispute claims on chargebacks.

---

### 5. Terms & Acceptance
- **Payment:** 50% deposit on kickoff, 50% upon completion of end-to-end testing in staging.
- **To proceed:** Reply with "Approved Option [1, 2, or 3]" and your billing contact. Kickoff will be scheduled within 24 hours.
```
